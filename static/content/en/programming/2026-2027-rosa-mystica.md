# Rosa Mystica

**A musical journey around the Virgin**

Two concerts in December:

**Friday 11 December 2026 at 8 pm**
Église Saint-Albert-le-Grand
122 rue de la Glacière, 75013 Paris (metro Glacière)

**Thursday 17 December 2026 at 8:30 pm**
Église Saint-Antoine des Quinze-Vingts
66 avenue Ledru-Rollin, 75012 Paris (metro Gare de Lyon and Ledru-Rollin)

Échappées Sonores invites you to Rosa Mystica, a musical journey around the Virgin. This programme highlights the feminine and maternal face of Mary, with tenderness, beauty, sorrow and consolation.

Conducted by Marilou Gonsseaume, with Ilaï Goldenberg at the piano, the women's vocal ensemble brings centuries and styles into dialogue. The programme runs from Hildegard of Bingen to the women composers of today, by way of Monteverdi, Pergolesi, Duruflé and Poulenc. Between contemplation and emotion, light and shadow, each work unveils one more petal of this mysterious rose.

Free admission subject to availability – voluntary contribution to costs.
We recommend arriving a little early.
